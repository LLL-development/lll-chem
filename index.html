How to play

1

Look at the atom counts below the equation — each shows left / right.

2

Use the − and + buttons to change the coefficients in front of each formula.

3

When every count matches (all green ✓), press Check. The simplest ratio scores most.

Why balance equations?

Atoms are never created or destroyed in a chemical reaction — they are only rearranged. That's the law of conservation of mass. A balanced equation shows the same number of each atom on both sides, so it describes what really happens.
2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O
